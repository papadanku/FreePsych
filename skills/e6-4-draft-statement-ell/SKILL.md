# Generate English Language Learner (ELL) Statement Narratives

## Purpose

This skill generates professional qualitative narratives for the English Language Learner (ELL) section of a psychoeducational report (Section E6, Other/EL). It articulates the rationale clarifying whether a student's need for individualized instruction stems from their educational disability rather than their English language proficiency.

## Keywords & Placeholders

You must use these exact variables throughout the narrative:

- `_firstname`
- `_boy/girl`
- `_he/she`, `_He/She`
- `_him/her`, `_Him/Her`
- `_his/her`, `_His/Her`

## Universal Rules

1. **Content Strategy**: Clearly distinguish between language acquisition/proficiency factors and the presence of a primary educational disability or medical diagnosis requiring special education support.
2. **Tense**: Use present or past tense as appropriate based on the narrative context.
3. **Tone**: Professional, objective, and academic.
4. **Voice**: Active voice, plain English.

## Narrative Templates & Examples

- `Although Spanish is _firstname's primary home language, _His/Her dominant language and primary language spoken is English. _firstname's need for individualized instruction is due to _his/her medical diagnosis and educational disability, not _his/her English language proficiency. According to _firstname's AZELLA test in 2017, _he/she was in the Basic range.`
- `_firstname was considered to be an EL student until May 2017, at which point _he/she was SPED withdrawn. _firstname's need for specialized instruction appears to be a result of _his/her disability, not _his/her language proficiency. _firstname has been instructed exclusively in English throughout _his/her academic career. Despite receiving interventions and special education services, _firstname has not shown the expected academic growth. _firstname's disability, rather than _his/her language proficiency, appears to be the primary cause of _his/her difficulties.`
- `_firstname's primary home language is English, so _he/she is not considered an English Language Learner (ELL) student. _firstname's need for individualized instruction is due to _his/her educational disability, not _his/her English language proficiency.`
- `_firstname's primary home language is English, so _he/she is not considered an English Language Learner (ELL) student. _His/Her continued need for individualized instruction is due to _his/her educational disability, not _his/her English language proficiency.`
- `Although _firstname's home language is English/Spanish, _he/she has spoken English and received English instruction this school year. _His/Her need for individualized instruction is due to _his/her educational disability, not _his/her English language proficiency.`
