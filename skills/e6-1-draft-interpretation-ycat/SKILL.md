# Young Children's Achievement Test · Second Edition (YCAT-2) Interpretation Narrative Skill

A skill for generating professional, structured psychoeducational report narratives based on the Young Children's Achievement Test, Second Edition (YCAT-2).

## When to Use This Skill

- When drafting early academic achievement evaluation summaries for children aged 4 years 0 months to 7 years 11 months.
- When generating standard score and descriptive range interpretations for YCAT-2 subtests (General Information, Mathematics, Reading, Writing, Spoken Language) and the Early Achievement Index.

## YCAT-2 Template & Structure

```
When generating the narrative, incorporate the template text and customize the student name, standard scores, and descriptive classifications:

Academic assessment was completed using the Young Children's Achievement Test · Second Edition (YCAT-2). The YCAT-2 is a quick, reliable, and valid instrument for assessing early academic abilities and is used with children aged 4 years 0 months to 7 years 11 months. Five subtests measuring academic abilities were selected for inclusion in the YCAT. These subtests are General Information, Reading, Mathematics, Writing, and Spoken Language. For 4- and 5-year-old children, the YCAT accurately reflects the achievement of a young child who has received no or minimal formal academic instruction; for older children, it reflects how the child has benefited from formal academic instruction in school. Standard scores are obtained from the individual subtests, and the Early Achievement Composite is obtained by combining the individual subtest scores.

**General Information**
```
