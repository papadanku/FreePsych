# Assistive Technology Evaluation Summary Statements

## Purpose

This skill generates professional narratives for the Assistive Technology section of psychoeducational evaluation reports, documenting whether the student requires assistive devices or technology services to access and progress in the general curriculum.

## Keywords & Placeholders

You must use these exact variables throughout the narrative:

- `_firstname`
- `_boy/girl`
- `_he/she`, `_He/She`
- `_him/her`, `_Him/Her`
- `_his/her`, `_His/Her`

## Universal Rules

1. **Content Strategy**: Document the multidisciplinary team's findings regarding assistive technology needs and access to universal district devices.
2. **Tense**: Present tense.
3. **Tone**: Professional, objective, and academic.
4. **Voice**: Active voice, plain English.

## Narrative Templates & Examples

- `_firstname does not present a profile consistent with extraordinary special needs for technology and assistive devices. However, _firstname will have access to assistive devices geared to promote learning and academic skill development, which will be available to all Liberty Elementary School District students.`
- `The team decided that _firstname does not need assistive technology to access the general curriculum at this time.`
