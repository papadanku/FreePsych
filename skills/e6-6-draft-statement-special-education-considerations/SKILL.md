# Special Education Considerations and Service Modifications

## Purpose

This skill generates professional narratives for psychoeducational reports regarding whether any additions or modifications to special education or related services are needed for the student to progress in the general curriculum.

## Keywords & Placeholders

You must use these exact variables throughout the narrative:

- `_firstname`
- `_boy/girl`
- `_he/she`, `_He/She`
- `_him/her`, `_Him/Her`
- `_his/her`, `_His/Her`

## Universal Rules

1. **Content Strategy**: Document service minutes adjustments, instructional accommodations, related services additions, and continuation or modification of IEP goals and placement.
2. **Tense**: Present and past tense as appropriate.
3. **Tone**: Professional, objective, and academic.
4. **Voice**: Active voice, plain English.

## Narrative Templates & Examples

- `_firstname has been receiving special education services in the resource classroom and progressing in this setting. However, based on the evaluation results, the team would like to consider increasing _firstname's service minutes to provide _him/her with further, specially designed academic instruction to address areas of need (Written Expression, Basic Reading Skills, Math Computation, Reading Comprehension, and Reading Fluency).`
- `Utilize visual modalities when introducing new and/or old concepts to _firstname to help _him/her better encode/learn the material being presented. Teach _firstname to use context clues to identify words and phrases. Highlighting important vocabulary for each class in each subject area will help increase _his/her comprehension and learning of the material being presented to _him/her in class. When needed, provide extra time on any timed and/or untimed tests/quizzes and/or assignments to help accommodate _his/her lower processing and fluency abilities. Provide _firstname with class notes when available so _he/she can follow instructions better. Have _firstname highlight reading materials as _he/she reads to help with _his/her comprehension and note-taking skills (when class notes are available).`
- `_firstname has been receiving special education services in the resource classroom and has been making progress in this setting. However, based on the evaluation results, the team would like to consider increasing _firstname's service minutes to provide _him/her with further, specially designed academic instruction to address areas of need. The team also believes that adding counseling services to _firstname's IEP will aid in developing _firstname's social skills.`
- `The IEP team should continue the academic goals developed in November 2021 to address _firstname's learning needs. The team should consider continuing goals in _firstname's basic reading skills, reading fluency, reading comprehension, math problem-solving, and written expression.`
- `_firstname benefits from continuing modifications/accommodations to support _his/her academic deficits and impulsive behaviors in the general education classroom. _firstname has demonstrated growth across _his/her academic and social-emotional goals outlined in _his/her Individualized Education Program (IEP).`
- `_firstname may benefit from receiving educational services in a self-contained classroom with modifications/accommodations to support academic deficits and behavior needs. _firstname's special education eligibility remains the same with Autism, Mild Intellectual Disability (secondary), and Speech-Language Impairment (secondary).`
- `_firstname benefits from the continuation of modifications/accommodations to support _his/her academic deficits in the general education classroom. Based on the results of this re-evaluation, additions or modifications to the current IEP will be made. In the general education classroom, preferential seating and sitting next to a helpful student are needed.`
