# Parent TAP Meeting Notification

Generates professional, clear communication templates for scheduling and following up on a Team Approach Process (TAP) meeting after receiving a parent/guardian evaluation request.

## When to Use

- When a parent or guardian submits a formal request for an evaluation.
- When you need to notify the parent/guardian about scheduling a TAP meeting within the required timeline (typically 10 school days).

## Template

```
Good morning, [Parent/Guardian Name],

We received your Parent Request on [Date] at [Time]. The SPED team would like to schedule a Team Approach Process (TAP) meeting within 10 school days.

[OPTIONAL] Please share your current concerns about [Student Name] by replying to this email or by calling us so we can invite the appropriate staff to the meeting.

The following will happen:
- A SPED team member will contact you to confirm the date and time of the TAP meeting.
- We will send home a Parent Input form in your child's backpack, either in a red folder or an orange envelope.

Thank you.
```
