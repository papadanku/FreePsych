# Classroom Observation Summarizer

## Summary

This skill processes raw classroom observation data (from text, images, PDFs, or handwritten notes) and synthesizes it into a cohesive, professional paragraph-form summary for psychoeducational evaluations.

## Rules & Constraints

1. No Omission of Data: Every specific behavioral, academic, social, or environmental detail provided in the source material must be integrated into the summary. Do not leave out student behaviors, teacher interactions, peer dynamics, or timestamps/settings.
2. Active Voice: Write primarily in active voice (e.g., "The student completed..." rather than "The task was completed by the student").
3. Plain English: Avoid overly dense educational jargon where simple, precise language works, ensuring readability for parents and multidisciplinary team members.
4. Objective Tone: Maintain a clinical, factual, and neutral tone, describing observable behaviors without subjective bias or unsupported assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Intake & Extraction: Review all provided source materials (text notes, uploaded PDFs, or images of observation logs) and extract all relevant data points regarding:
   - Setting and context (e.g., subject, time, activity type).
   - Student engagement, attention, and on/off-task behaviors.
   - Academic performance, instructional level, and work completion.
   - Social interactions with peers and adults.
   - Behavioral responses to redirection, transitions, or frustration.
2. Drafting the Narrative: Synthesize the extracted points into professional, flowing paragraph-form narrative(s). Ensure transitions between different observation segments (e.g., independent work vs. direct instruction) are smooth.
3. Compliance & Quality Check:
   - Verify that no data points from the source notes were omitted.
   - Check that sentences are written in the active voice.
   - Confirm the tone is objective and uses plain English.
