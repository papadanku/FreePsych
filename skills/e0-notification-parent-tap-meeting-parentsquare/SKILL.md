# Parent TAP Meeting Notification Template

This skill provides a standardized, professional communication template for notifying parents or guardians about scheduling a Team Approach Process (TAP) meeting following a formal parent request for an evaluation.

## When to Use

Use this template when responding to a parent or guardian's written request for an evaluation to initiate the Team Approach Process (TAP) within 10 school days.

## Template Content

```
Good morning, [Parent/Guardian Name],
—
We received your Parent Request on [Date] at [Time]. The SPED team would like to schedule a Team Approach Process (TAP) meeting within 10 school days.
—
[OPTIONAL] Please share your current concerns about _firstname by replying to this email or by calling us so we can invite the appropriate staff to the meeting.
—
The following will happen:
- A SPED team member will contact you to confirm the date and time of the TAP meeting.
- We will send home a Parent Input form in your child's backpack, either in a red folder or an orange envelope.
—
Thank you.
```

## Instructions for Use

1. Replace placeholder bracketed text (e.g., `[Parent/Guardian Name]`, `[Date]`, `[Time]`, `_firstname`) with the specific student and communication details.
2. Include or remove the `[OPTIONAL]` section based on whether parent concern details have already been gathered.
3. Ensure contact and follow-up steps are clearly communicated to the family.
