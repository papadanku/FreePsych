# Woodcock-Johnson IV Tests of Oral Language Interpretation Drafting

## Summary

This skill generates professional narrative interpretations for oral language abilities evaluated via the Woodcock-Johnson IV Tests of Oral Language (WJ-IV OL) based on the Cattell-Horn-Carroll (CHC) model and the associated clusters and subtests.

## When to Use

Use when drafting psychoeducational evaluation reports incorporating WJ-IV oral language assessment results across clusters and subtests, including Oral Language, Broad Oral Language, Oral Expression, Listening Comprehension, Phonetic Coding, and Speed of Lexical Access.

## Steps

1. Review individual cluster and subtest scores from the WJ-IV Tests of Oral Language assessment.
2. Populate domain-specific narrative templates for Oral Language, Broad Oral Language, Oral Expression, Listening Comprehension, Phonetic Coding, and Speed of Lexical Access.
3. Include standard scores (SS) and percentile ranks (PR) for each domain.
