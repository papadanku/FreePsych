
# Comprehensive Test of Nonverbal Intelligence · Second Edition (CTONI-2) Interpretation Drafting

## Summary

This skill generates professional narrative interpretations for nonverbal intelligence and reasoning evaluated via the CTONI-2 assessment.

## When to Use

Use when drafting psychoeducational reports incorporating CTONI-2 Full Scale, Pictorial, and Geometric composite scores.

## Steps

1. Review standard scores for CTONI-2 Full Scale, Pictorial Scale, and Geometric Scale.
2. Populate domain narrative templates with student-specific names and performance ranges.
3. Analyze potential discrepancies between pictorial and geometric reasoning abilities.
